What is MindSpace?

A calm room, a gentle garden, and someone to talk to.

MindSpace is a 3D exploration game played in first person and designed around one idea: sometimes it helps to say things out loud. There are no enemies, no timers and no scores. Just a sunlit room with sage walls, an open door to a garden, and voice companions ready to listen.

You speak into your microphone. Your companion hears you, thinks, and replies in a natural, streamed voice with gentle captions, powered by ElevenLabs conversational agents.

  • Explore at your own pace with no head bob, camera shake or flashing lights
  • Rabbits, squirrels and songbirds go about their day outside
  • Rain, clouds, starry nights and the northern lights, all at a whisper

How it works

Talk, don’t type.

Every conversation is voice in and voice out, so it feels less like a chatbot and more like sitting with someone.

  1. You speak

    Sit down near a companion and just talk. The microphone only opens after you say yes on the consent screen.

  2. They listen & think

    An ElevenLabs agent turns your words into text and writes a thoughtful, supportive reply in real time.

  3. They answer out loud

    The reply streams back as a natural voice from your companion, with soft captions timed to their words.

Inside the game

Small, soft details that add up to calm.

A room that listens

Talk from anywhere in the cabin: standing, sitting or by the window. Step outside with the door open and the voice follows, fading gently with distance.

Grounding corners

A breathing orb that swells over ten seconds, a grounding shelf of stones, plants and a journal, and a reflection seat by the lake window.

A living garden

Follow the flower path around a pond and over a little bridge. Rabbits graze, squirrels scamper, songbirds rest and butterflies drift by.

Gentle weather

A slow 12 minute day drifts from afternoon light to starry night. Soft rain comes and goes, or choose clear skies whenever you like.

Quiet music

A tiny lofi player with a slowly spinning record keeps you company. Skip, pause or go silent any time with [ P ].

Calm by design

No motion blur, vignette, head bob or sudden effects. A minimal HUD, readable captions, and an option for larger captions.

Care & privacy

Built with care, and honest about what it is.

You choose when to talk

Every play session starts by asking. Choose Play without voice and the microphone is never touched. Mute or end a conversation at any moment.

Nothing is saved by the game

MindSpace doesn’t write transcripts or recordings to disk. Voice is processed by ElevenLabs during the conversation, under their own policies.

A companion, not a clinician

MindSpace is a wellness and reflection space. It isn’t therapy, medical advice or an emergency service.

If you need to talk to a person right now

In Canada or the United States, call or text 988 at any time, day or night. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential support near you. If you’re in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
